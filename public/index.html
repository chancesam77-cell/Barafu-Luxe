Gemstone Sourcing
Barafu Luxe · Sourced at the Source
Found Nowhere
Else on Earth.
Tanzanite exists in one place on the planet — the foothills of Kilimanjaro, an hour from Arusha. Barafu Luxe connects members directly with licensed gem dealers for tanzanite and Tanzania's other rare stones, sourced, certified, and arranged entirely through your concierge.
How sourcing works
1. Tell us what you're after
Stone type, size, budget, and whether it's a loose stone or a finished piece.
2. We source through licensed Arusha dealers
Direct relationships with vetted gem traders — the same market that supplies major international jewelry houses.
3. Certification & valuation
GIA or IGI certification arranged on request, so you know exactly what you're holding.
4. Delivered to you
At your villa, hotel, or arranged for collection — whatever suits your itinerary.

Real Estate Concierge
Barafu Luxe · Off-Market Access
Property, Introduced
Not Advertised.
East Africa's luxury property market rarely reaches public listings — the best beachfront leaseholds and northern-circuit estates move through private introductions. Barafu Luxe connects members with licensed local agents and investment counsel to explore what's genuinely available.
A note on ownership
Foreign nationals cannot own land outright on mainland Tanzania — land is held by the state, with access structured through long-term leaseholds or Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) derivative rights. Zanzibar operates under separate, more accessible rules via ZIPA-approved developments. Barafu Luxe is a concierge and introduction service, not a licensed broker or law firm — every purchase is conducted through independent, licensed Tanzanian legal counsel and registered agents that we introduce you to.
How it works
1. Tell us what you're looking for
Region, budget, and purpose — primary residence, investment, or development.
2. We introduce you to licensed local partners
Registered real estate agents and TIC-experienced legal counsel, matched to your region of interest.
3. Private viewings arranged
Off-market properties, viewed discreetly, with transport and scheduling handled by your concierge.
4. Ongoing management available
Once acquired, Barafu Luxe can arrange property management, staffing, and maintenance.
